How I can better help the team as a Principal Designer

The short version

Where we are

Our ambitions point towards spanning many teams, clients and services, Design can help bring cohesion to the visions before delivery.

What I bring

High level systems thinking, information architecture, service and product design and research, with all of the low level execution capabilities to match.

Where I’d like to go

Bring design in earlier across our data products, as Principal Designer.

In Lena’s own words

“Software & connected devices create intelligence and enables action at scale.”

Why SE formed the Software & Services Division

Customers increasingly need integrated solutions (not disconnected capabilities) to solve complex energy and operational challenges.

AI-native Energy Intelligence creates the opportunity to scale domain expertise, automate decisions and continuously improve performance.

Bringing Advisory, Software and Services together connects insight, action and execution across the customer lifecycle.

Everything’s moving towards an integrated, value generating ecosystem. Why should our software be different?

What this means, and how design ties in

Early cohesion

Design can help map the whole system, so no team solves its piece alone.

Upstream concepting

Design can help set the foundations early, concepting at a unified vision level.

Big instead of little picture

Design can help product direction focus on purpose instead of features and use cases.

I’ve also done this before

At TransPerfect

Led design for both client and internal facing tools, establishing and executing Research, Design, and entire process in-between at $1.4b company

- Client portal structural design: scaling revenue from $0.31M to $240M in 10 years, Converted at 10 times the rate of other similar products in the company

- Internal project management portal design: Single-handedly designed product that now runs entire company production workflow. Voluntary adoption rate of 85% of entire company user base in year 1 of launch
